You

RocketPlan

The evidence adjusters ask for, labelled, before you leave the site.

On site

Log atmospheric and moisture readings

You

Each visit, log temperature, humidity and moisture in RocketDry. A photo of the meter travels with every reading.

RocketPlan

Builds the drying log per room per day, flags a missed day, and shows when each room reaches dry standard.

You walk away with

Proof of drying nobody can argue with.

Material, reading, meter photo and the drying goal on one screen. On the sample job the Home Office reaches dry standard on September 6, day 4; the Rec Room's missing day-4 reading is flagged in the package.
